# Level Downloads

Want to keep a level for later, even when you have no internet? This mod lets you download any level and play it offline whenever you like.

## What you get

- **A Level Downloads button** in the Creator menu, right next to Create. It opens your downloaded levels.
- **A download button on every level page**, next to refresh. Press it and the level gets saved together with its 20 newest and 20 top comments.
- **A gray badge** in the same spot once a level is saved, so you can see at a glance that you already have it.
- **Your own Downloaded Levels screen.** It looks like a normal level list. Levels open offline with no redownload, and the little trash button deletes them.
- **Comments you can read offline**, with usernames and likes. Only the saved 20 newest and 20 top are there, so the next page is empty.

## Good to know

- Downloads are saved as `<level name>.gmd` files in this mod's config folder.
- Every file starts with the comments, then the level data.
