# Level Downloads

- A **Level Downloads** button in the Creator menu, next to Create.
- A download button on every level page, under like/dislike. It saves the level, the 20 most recent and the 20 top comments.
- Your own **Downloaded Levels** screen. Levels open offline, with no redownload. The saved comments can be read offline.

Files are saved as `<level name>.gmd` in this mod's config folder, in the format
`{<20 recent comments>#<20 top comments>}#<gmd text>#`.
